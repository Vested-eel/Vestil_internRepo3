## Data Privacy & Confidentiality Reflection

**Steps I can take to ensure secure handling of data:**

- Always use company-approved tools for storing and sharing files
- Double-check permissions before sharing documents
- Keep devices password-protected and updated

**Safe storage, sharing, and disposal practices:**

- Store files in encrypted drives or secure cloud systems
- Share only via authorized platforms (no personal email or messaging apps)
- Permanently delete files when no longer needed

**Common mistakes to avoid:**

- Leaving sensitive files open or unattended
- Using weak or repeated passwords
- Sharing confidential information casually in conversations or emails
