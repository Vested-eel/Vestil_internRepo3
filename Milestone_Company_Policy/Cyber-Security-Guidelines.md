# Cyber Security Reflection:

## What security measures do you currently follow, and where can you improve?

- At the moment, I rely on the default settings of my laptop's automatic updates' built-in anti-virus and a password protected login. However, I've yet to use a password manager and two-time authentication enabled on all accounts that require it. I also forget to lock my computer sometimes and I have been developing the habit of locking it should I walk away.

## How can you make secure behavior a habit rather than an afterthought?

- I will treat security steps as part of my daily routine. For example, I’ll make locking my computer automatic whenever I leave my desk (using shortcuts like Windows + L). I’ll also commit to checking for suspicious emails before clicking links, so it becomes second nature rather than something I only do when reminded.

## What steps will you take to ensure your passwords and accounts are secure?

- Set-up a password manager to generate and store strong passwords.

- Enable 2FA on my work and personal accounts.

- Regular checkups on all accounts and emails sent and received.

- Avoid reusing similar password and change already similar existing password.

## What would you do if you suspected a security breach or suspicious activity on your account?

- I would immediately change my password, enable or re-check 2FA, and disconnect from the network if necessary. Then I’d report the incident to the IT/security team at Focus Bear. If malware is suspected, I’d run a full system scan and follow company protocols for incident response.
