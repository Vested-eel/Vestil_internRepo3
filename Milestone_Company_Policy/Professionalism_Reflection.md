## How can you help create a positive, fun, and professional work environment?

By being friendly whilst being professional on the job and not be abrasive towards my fellow co-workers.

## How can you prepare effectively for meetings with supervisors and colleagues?

- By taking notes on deadlines and taking notes on important tasks/dates from the previous meetings.

## What would it look like to be proactive during your internship?

- By not just being idle, taking the initiative or asks fellow colleagues for tasks they need help with.

## How might you follow up with colleagues or supervisors if you need information/action from them? How would you escalate it further if you don't get a response and when would you escalate?

- I would ask them via email first, prompting for a response as soon as possible. I would write with urgency
  and having key words that would be read immediately and catches the attention of my colleague. I would escalate
  when considerable time has passed, or an imminent deadline is approaching.
