# 🤖 AI Usage Guidelines

### 🎯 Goal

To understand how to responsibly and effectively use AI tools while maintaining data privacy and critical thinking.

---

## 🔍 Research & Learn

**1. AI tools typically used in my role**

- Drafting and editing written content (emails, reports, documentation)
- Brainstorming ideas or generating outlines
- Summarizing information and creating study notes
- Automating repetitive tasks (e.g., formatting, code snippets)

**2. Benefits and risks of using AI in a professional setting**

- _Benefits:_ Increased productivity, faster drafting, idea generation, reduced workload.
- _Risks:_ Potential inaccuracies, over-reliance on AI, data privacy breaches if sensitive information is shared.

**3. Information that should never be entered into AI tools**

- Confidential company data (financials, internal documents, client information)
- Personal identifiable information (PII) such as addresses, phone numbers, or IDs
- Proprietary code or trade secrets

**4. Fact-checking and validating AI-generated content**

- Cross-check with reliable sources or official documentation
- Review for accuracy, tone, and professionalism
- Edit outputs to ensure they align with company standards and context

---

## 📝 Reflection

**When to use AI vs. my own skills:**

- Use AI for brainstorming, drafting, or summarizing.
- Rely on my own skills for final decision-making, critical analysis, and tasks requiring judgment or confidentiality.

**Avoiding over-reliance on AI:**

- Treat AI as a supportive tool, not a replacement for expertise.
- Always review and refine outputs before using them.
- Continue building my own skills alongside AI assistance.

**Steps to ensure data privacy:**

- Never input confidential or sensitive data into AI tools.
- Use AI only for general or non-sensitive tasks.
- Follow company policies on approved tools and secure usage.
