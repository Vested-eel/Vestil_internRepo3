# Help Strategy Reflection

**When do I prefer using AI vs Google?**

- I use AI when I need step-by-step guidance, explanations, or quick debugging help. I use Google when I want official documentation, community answers, or multiple perspectives.

**How do I decide when to ask a colleague instead?**

- I ask a colleague when the issue is project-specific, sensitive, or urgent. Colleagues provide context that AI or Google cannot, especially when the problem involves team workflows or company code.

**What challenges do developers face when troubleshooting alone?**

- Information overload from search results.
- Risk of misunderstanding AI-generated code.
- Wasting time on trial-and-error without guidance.
- Difficulty handling urgent blockers without team support.
