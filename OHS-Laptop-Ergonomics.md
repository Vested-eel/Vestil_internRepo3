# Occupational Health & Safety (OHS) for Desk-Based Work / Reflection

## Equipment changes I can make:

- Use a laptop stand to raise the screen.

- Connect an external keyboard and mouse.

- Adjust my chair to ensure proper back s.upport

## Behavioral changes I can implement:

- Sit upright with shoulders relaxed.

- Take regular breaks to move and stretch.

- Adjust screen height to avoid looking down for long periods.

## Reminders to maintain good habits:

- Set digital reminders or use apps (e.g., Focus Bear) to schedule movement breaks.

- Place a sticky note near my desk as a visual cue for posture checks.

- Incorporate short stretching routines into my daily schedule.
