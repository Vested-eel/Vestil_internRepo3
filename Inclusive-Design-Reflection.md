# Reflection

## Adjusting my communication style:

- Use clear, concise language without unnecessary jargon
- Provide step-by-step guidance when explaining tasks
- Be patient and open to clarifying questions

## Common pitfalls to avoid:

- Overloading users with too much information at once
- Using vague or indirect communication that may cause confusion
- Designing interfaces that require multitasking or rapid switching

## Practical change I can make:

- Break down complex tasks into smaller, manageable steps in both my work and communication
- Incorporate reminders or cues to support focus and reduce frustration
